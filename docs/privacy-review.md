# Privacy Review Checklist

- [ ] Confirm this is employee activity metadata and identify the lawful internal purpose.
- [ ] Confirm who can see aggregate reports.
- [ ] Confirm who can see per-user details.
- [ ] Confirm whether per-user CSV export is allowed.
- [ ] Confirm retention period.
- [ ] Confirm excluded identities, such as break-glass or service accounts.
- [ ] Confirm whether emails should be displayed, pseudonymized, or hidden.
- [ ] Confirm regional storage requirements.
